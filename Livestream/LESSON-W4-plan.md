## Part 1: Paraphrasing (≤ 25 min), decided

### 1.1 Warm-up poll: "Paraphrase là gì?" (~2 min)
- **Teacher:** fires the poll, reveals the result, and makes the point that synonyms are not the only way and are not reliable.
- **Student:** taps one answer. Options **(draft, anh Khoa to edit):**
  - A. Dùng từ đồng nghĩa (synonyms)
  - B. Nói lại cùng ý theo cách khác để không lặp từ
  - C. Viết câu dài hơn cho hay hơn
  - D. Em chưa rõ
- **Board:** the class result as a bar chart.

### 1.2 Bad paraphrase: right or wrong? (1–2 items, ~4 min)
- **Teacher:** shows a sentence pair. Students vote; anh Khoa explains by talking. Short.
- **Student:** taps **Đúng** / **Sai**.
- Items **(draft sentences, based on anh Khoa's examples):**
  1. "Providing free **housing** helps low-income families. Free **houses** also reduce homelessness." → Sai: "houses" doesn't cover what "housing" means (synonym = risky).
  2. "The city plans a new public **building**. This **structure** will …" → Sai: "structure" misses the main topic.
- No anh Đậu here.

### 1.3 Three ways that work (teacher only, ~6 min)
- anh Khoa paraphrases the free-housing model paragraph live. No student activity.
- The board shows the 3 methods (from his notes):
  1. **Pronouns:** it / they; "the red bag / the blue **one**"
  2. **Reference:** **this** (= cái này) · **such** + noun (= this kind of … = những cái giống vậy) · **do so**
  3. **Generalisation:** this practice / this policy / this provision …
- He closes by saying: "You won't be tested on naming the method. These are tools." No check question.

### 1.4 Exercise 1: six items (~12 min)
- **Teacher:**
  - fires one item at a time;
  - sees the answers **grouped by wording**, with anh Đậu's verdict and a **copy button** on each group;
  - pastes an interesting answer onto his board himself (e.g. "using them" to teach the former / the latter);
  - can **flip a verdict** with one click, which is saved for next time.
- **Student:** types the replacement for the bold phrase.
- **anh Đậu:** **✓ / ✓ but… / ✗ + one quick explanation**, no back-and-forth.
- **Board:** the item, the timer, and how many have answered.

| # | Bold phrase (paraphrase it in the next sentence) | ✓ Accepted (anh Khoa's worksheet) | Notes for anh Đậu |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | importing food from other parts of the world | doing so, this practice, it, this, such imports | "it" = the importation: fine |
| 2 | a ban on plastic bags | this policy, this measure, such a ban, this ban, it, doing so | "doing so" = placing a ban (from the sentence before) |
| 3 | using single-use plastic bags *(worksheet says "use"; typo)* | this practice, doing so, this trend, this tendency, using the former | ✗ "using them": could mean the other kinds of bags → **teach the former / the latter** |
| 4 | focusing on academic qualifications | focusing on the former, doing so, focusing on such qualifications, this trend, this tendency | **teach the first / the second / the last** (for 3 things) |
| 5 | having children later in life | doing so, this practice, this trend, this tendency, it | |
| 6 | Imported foods | such food, it | ✓ but… "they": the sentence before says "imported **food**" (uncountable) → "it" fits better |

**anh Đậu's rules for all items (set by anh Khoa):**
1. The reference must point to **one** clear thing.
2. **Singular / plural must match what it points back to in the sentence before**, not the phrase being replaced. "It" is fine unless that thing is plural.
3. A generalisation must cover the **same** thing, not more and not less.
4. The grammar must fit the sentence (e.g. a gerund subject).
5. An answer not on the list but following the rules → accept. Unsure → "Có thể đúng, anh Khoa sẽ xem" and flag it to the teacher.

## Part 2: Type 3 & Type 4, a Week 3 revision (**draft**, ~? min)

**Core idea (anh Khoa):** "To what extent", "positive or negative" and "advantages outweigh disadvantages" are **the same question**: weigh the advantages against the disadvantages. Agree / positive = the advantages are bigger. The **40/60** framework works for all opinion questions: the smaller side goes in Body 1, the bigger side in Body 2.

### 2.1 Quick revision check (draft)
- **Poll:** "Đề hỏi *Is this a positive or negative development?* Em nói 'positive' nghĩa là…" → *Lợi lớn hơn hại* / *Không có hại* / *Chỉ nói lợi*.
- **Poll:** "Body 1 trong 40/60 viết về…" → *Bên nhỏ (bên mình không chọn)* / *Bên lớn*.

### 2.2 Type 3 topic: businesses moving to rural areas
Topic: *As transport and accommodation problems are increasing in many large cities, some governments are encouraging businesses to move to rural areas. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*

**Steps (from anh Khoa's notes):**
1. Read the topic carefully and find the **modifiers** that limit the argument.
2. Choose a view and apply 40/60.
3. Brainstorm core ideas + development.

**Activities (draft):**
- **Step 1, tap the modifier:** students tap the words in the topic that limit the argument. Answer: "*As transport and accommodation problems are increasing in many large cities*". The board shows the class heat map.
- **Step 2, pick a view:** students tap **Lợi > Hại** / **Hại > Lợi**. The board shows the split.
- **Step 3, brainstorm by group:**
  - Students tap a group chip (**Workers · Family · Company · Government · Environment · Society**) and send a short idea.
  - The teacher sees the ideas grouped and picks some for the board.
  - anh Đậu: ✓ / ✓ but… / ✗ on "is this really an advantage / disadvantage of moving businesses?"
- **Paraphrasing link:** the topic itself is paraphrased in his notes (businesses → companies; move → relocate / migrate; rural areas → the countryside / such areas). Reuse it as a quick "which of these is a safe paraphrase?" (draft).
- **Model (anh Khoa's notes):**
  - Intro: "It is common for the authorities to promote the relocation of businesses to the countryside …"
  - Thesis: "Although **this policy** …, they are far outweighed by …" (paraphrasing in action).
  - Body 1 topic sentence: "Admittedly, there may be some minor advantages when companies migrate to rural areas."
  - Body 2 topic sentence: "However, I believe the disadvantages stemming from **this migration** are far more significant."

⚠️ **To check with anh Khoa:** his steps say **advantages win** (Lợi >>> Hại, Body 2 = "lợi đáng kể hơn"), but the model intro, Body 2 and conclusion argue **disadvantages win**. Which way does he teach it?

**anh Khoa's brainstorm (seed ideas for anh Đậu and the "Tìm lỗi" waiting game):**
- Disadvantages:
  - Workers: moving costs; staying in the city → harder job search; rural → lower living standard, less access to facilities.
  - Family: children's education quality, or split families.
  - Company: harder to recruit, relocation cost, fewer customers.
  - Government: tax cuts / subsidies + infrastructure → strain on the budget.
  - Environment: deforestation for new infrastructure → loss of wildlife habitats.
- Advantages:
  - Workers: lower living costs.
  - Company: lower operating costs.
  - Society: a smaller development gap between cities and the countryside.
  - **Lower population density in big cities → fixes transport and accommodation** (highlighted in his notes: the answer to the topic's own modifier).

## Part 3: Conclusion (**draft**, ~? min)

**Formula (worksheet):**
- **Sentence 1:** summarise the 2 bodies (40/60): "X có vài cái hại nhỏ nhưng cái lợi lớn hơn".
- **Sentence 2:** re-answer the question, depending on the type:
  - Type 2 (To what extent): agree / disagree.
  - Type 3: do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? (Not needed if it would repeat sentence 1.)
  - Type 4: positive or negative trend. *"Based on the aforementioned arguments, I contend that X is an overall positive / negative trend."*

**Activities (draft):**
1. **Spot the parts:** students tap sentence 1 vs sentence 2 in one model conclusion (the worksheet has 3 intro + conclusion pairs: tobacco ban, watching TV alone, advertising).
2. **Writing task:**
   - Students write the conclusion for the Type 4 topic, children's freedom. anh Khoa's model: "In conclusion, while I acknowledge that granting children more freedom may be somewhat harmful, the benefits in terms of X and Y are far more impactful. Therefore, I believe this is a positive development."
   - anh Đậu: **direct** feedback, all errors + why → one check question.
   - Error order **(draft, anh Khoa to confirm):**
     1. no clear answer to the question;
     2. sentence 1 doesn't summarise both sides (40/60);
     3. sides reversed (the bigger side not clear);
     4. a sentence 2 that doesn't fit the question type;
     5. the topic phrase repeated instead of paraphrased (links back to Part 1);
     6. grammar;
     7. word choice.
3. **Early finishers:** the waiting screen (review cards from Part 1 + "Tìm lỗi của các bạn" using real class answers).

## Recap (end of class, draft)
1. Paraphrasing ≠ synonyms → **pronouns, reference (this / such / do so), generalisation**.
2. "It" must match what it points back to; use **the former / the latter** when "them" is unclear.
3. To what extent = positive / negative = advantages vs disadvantages → **40/60**.
4. Conclusion = **summary of both bodies + re-answer the question**.
