#### 5. How to write Conclusion

**[Conclusion:]{.underline}** Summarize 2 body paragraphs + Re-answer
the question

**Sentence 1: Tóm 2 body (40-60)** X có vài cái hại nhỏ nhưng cái lợi

***Example**: Based on the aforementioned arguments, I contend that X is

> ***Smoking is a major cause of serious illness and death throughout
> the world today. In the interest of public health, governments should
> ban cigarettes and other tobacco products. To what extent do you agree
> or disagree?***

Opinions are divided on whether the government should impose a complete
ban on tobacco products. I believe that **they should** because although
there may be some short-term advantages to smoking, they are far
outweighed by the long-term health problems.

In conclusion, although the nicotine found in tobacco products brings
immediate but short-term relief for people, the long-term consequences
of smoking for the willing and unwilling participants are far too
harmful. For the aforementioned reasons, I contend that the authorities
should place a ban on all tobacco products.

> ***People these days watch TV, films and other programmes alone rather
> than with other people. Do the advantages of this development outweigh
> the disadvantages?***

It is common these days for TV audiences to enjoy this pastime by
themselves instead of with their friends or family. Although this
tendency could create certain problems, I believe they are far
outweighed by the benefits of doing so.

In conclusion, even though the tendency of enjoying TV programs alone
might involve certain problems, I believe its benefits in terms of the
flexibility in determining the time and what to watch are more
significant.

> ***Advertisements are becoming more and more common in our everyday
> life. Is it a positive or negative development?***

Advertising has become an integral part of our daily life and become
more popular. I think this is a positive trend, because athough it may
bring about certain problems, the associated benefits brought far
outweight them.

In conclusion, even though advertising may indeed bring certain
inconveniences to its audience, the benefits in terms of providing
information and employment are more impactful and meaningful. Based on
the aforementioned arguments, I contend that the increasing prevalence
of advertising is an overall positive trend.
